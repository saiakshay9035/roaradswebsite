Common Questions

Everything brands ask before booking

How does a campaign work?

You send us your brand creative. We load it on the LED display, ride through your target areas during peak traffic, and send you GPS-verified proof-of-play footage the same day.

What's the minimum campaign duration?

We recommend a minimum of 3 days to see meaningful reach. Most brands start with a 1-week pilot to test the format before scaling.

How do I track my campaign?

Every ride is GPS-tracked. You get a daily report with routes covered, hours active, and footage from the ride. ERMS also monitors brand sentiment shifts during your campaign.

What areas do you cover in Bengaluru?

MG Road, Koramangala, Indiranagar, HSR Layout, Whitefield, and Electronic City corridors. Custom routes available on request.

What creative format do you need?

MP4 video (10-30 seconds), 16:9 ratio, minimum 1080p. We can also help with basic creative if needed.

Are you operational right now?

We're in beta testing phase. Founding brand slots are being confirmed now. Book your campaign and we'll schedule your launch date personally.
